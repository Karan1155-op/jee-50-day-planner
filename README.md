# 🚀 JEE 50-Day Intensive Study Planner & Routine Tracker

An interactive, offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to complete the entire remaining JEE syllabus across Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry (Inorganic & Organic) in 50 structured days.

**Live Link:** [https://karan1155-op.github.io/jee-50-day-planner/](https://karan1155-op.github.io/jee-50-day-planner/)

---

## 📱 Features

- **📱 Installable Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Install directly to your Android or iPhone home screen with 1 tap.
- **⚡ 100% Offline Capability**: Uses a modern Service Worker cache to work without an internet connection once loaded.
- **🗓️ 50-Day Structured Routine**:
  - Balanced 3 to 4 lectures daily (1h 15m each, Physics 1h 30m).
  - Every 7th day reserved for structured PYQ solving, revision & formula recap.
- **🗂️ 4-Page Navigation**:
  - **🏠 Dashboard**: Visual progress ring, syllabus completion stats, hours studied, and chapter completion checklist.
  - **📅 Schedule**: Week-by-week interactive view with expandable day cards, checkable lectures, study time badges, and "Go to Today" auto-focus.
  - **📚 Chapters**: Subject-filtered chapter list (Physics, Maths, Inorganic, Organic) with per-chapter lecture checklists.
  - **📊 Analytics**: Visual pacing tracker (Ahead / On Track / Behind), weekly lecture distribution charts, and subject breakdown.
- **💾 Automatic Persistence**: All lecture completions are safely stored in browser `localStorage`.
- **🎉 Milestones & Confetti**: Celebratory animations when chapters or entire subjects are finished.

## 📲 How to Install on Your Phone

### Android (Chrome / Brave / Edge)
1. Open [https://karan1155-op.github.io/jee-50-day-planner/](https://karan1155-op.github.io/jee-50-day-planner/) in your browser.
2. Tap the **"📲 Install"** button in the top navbar, or tap the three dots **(⋮)** in the browser menu and select **"Install app"** / **"Add to Home screen"**.
3. Tap **Install**. The app will now launch full-screen from your app drawer and home screen.

### iPhone / iPad (Safari)
1. Open [https://karan1155-op.github.io/jee-50-day-planner/](https://karan1155-op.github.io/jee-50-day-planner/) in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button (**⎋**) at the bottom.
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"** (**➕**).
4. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.
